### [AWOK Dual C5 Touch](https://lab401.com/collections/flipper-zero/products/awok-dual-touch-c5)
#### INTRODUCTION
- The AWOK Dual Touch C5 is a purpose-built wifi wardriving device for the Flipper Zero. Device operations are managed via the touch-screen; logs are ingested and saved via the Flipper Zero. The on-board GPS receiver guarantees accurate mapping and timestamping of all operations - allowing auditors hands-off, automated operation once activated.

- Wardriving, or wifi mapping & analysis is a vital element of pentesting and security auditing.

- For red-teams, penetration testers & offensive security auditors:

- Pre-engagement reconnaissance: Assessment of client networks during authorized security audits
- Vulnerability assessment: Identify weak encryption (WEP, WPA) in client networks
- Evil twin detection: Find unauthorized access points mimicking legitimate networks
- Probe Request Sniffing: Identify a target's geographical centers of interest
- For corporate security and blue-teams:

- Rogue access point detection: Identify unauthorized WiFi devices employees plug into corporate networks
- Network perimeter auditing: Map your organization's WiFi footprint to ensure no sensitive SSIDs are visible outside buildings
- Compliance verification: Document that networks meet security standards (WPA3 adoption, hidden SSIDs where required)

### [Flipper Zero Video Games Module](https://lab401.com/collections/flipper-zero/products/flipper-zero-video-game-module)
#### INTRODUCTION

- The Flipper Zero Video Game Module unlocks endless entertainment and development possibilities for your Flipper Zero!

- Out of the box, mirror your Flipper's screen on your TV via the HDMI output cable, and thanks to the internal gyroscope module, interact with Flipper's games in an innovative way.

- Under the hood, the Video Game Module is powered by a Raspberry Pi RP2040: the same controller that is used in thousands of Open-Source projects, from Robotics, IoT, Home Automation, Retro-Gaming and much more. Also built in is a fully-accessible gyroscope: unlocking a completely new sensor for your Flipper.

- The Video Game Module is a fully standalone platform with the power of the Pico and its GPIO fully accessible - for all sorts of electronic, development and debugging applications. When plugged into the Flipper, your Flipper levels up with all the extra features.

**Video Out: See your Flipper Zero screen on your TV**
- Raspberry Pi RP2040 Microcontroller: Same as in Raspberry Pi Pico, allowing you to run Raspberry Pi Pico code
- Motion Sensor: Comes with an IMU that has a gyroscope and accelerometer for motion tracking
- Connectivity: Has a 14-pin GPIO for plugging in joysticks, sensors, and more
- Flexible Use: Works with Flipper Zero or on its own
- Open-Source Firmware: Updateable via Flipper Zero or a PC
